Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Pov Swap ("we", "us") handles information in Pov Swap Publisher (the "App") and on this website.

Who uses the App

The App is an internal tool. Only members of the Pov Swap team sign in to it, and they use it to publish videos to Pov Swap's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. The App is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When we connect our own accounts through each platform's official sign-in (OAuth), the App receives access tokens and basic account details, such as the account ID and display name. These are needed to publish. We request only these permissions:

  • YouTube (Google): youtube.upload, to upload videos to our channel.
  • TikTok: user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish, to post videos to our TikTok account.
  • Meta: page and Instagram publishing permissions, to post Reels to our Facebook Page and Instagram account.

The App does not read, collect or store personal information about viewers or other users of these platforms. This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

How we use and store it

Tokens are used for one purpose: uploading videos that a Pov Swap team member has approved, together with their titles, descriptions and hashtags. Tokens are kept on our private server, which only our team can access. We do not sell, rent or share them, and we do not use them for advertising.

Google API Services

Pov Swap's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the App's access at any time on the Google security settings page.

Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only while an account is connected. When an account is disconnected or access is revoked, its tokens are deleted from our server. To ask us to delete any data, email the address below. We reply within 7 days.

Creators

Our videos contain clips of creators who allow clipping, or of campaigns we joined, and every video credits its creator. To have a clip removed, email us with the link. We remove it within 48 hours.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
