About the Pov Swap app

Pov Swap Publisher is our internal tool. It cuts highlights from streams we are allowed to clip. A team member reviews each clip, and approved clips are uploaded to Pov Swap's own accounts through the official YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API and Meta Graph API.

Only the Pov Swap team signs in to the app. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data from viewers or other users. How the app handles account access is described in our Privacy Policy.

Creators

We clip creators who allow it, or campaigns we joined. If you are a creator and want a clip removed, email us and we will take it down within 48 hours.
